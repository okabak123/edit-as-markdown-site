1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Edit as Markdown for Jira (the "App") handles information. The App is an Atlassian Forge app that adds an "Edit as Markdown for Jira" action to Jira Cloud issues.

2. Summary

  • The App stores no personal data.
  • No data leaves Atlassian: the App has no servers of its own and makes no external network requests.
  • We run no analytics or tracking.
  • We never receive the content of your Jira site.

3. How the App works with your data

The App runs on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). When a user opens it on an issue, its code runs in that user's browser, inside a sandboxed frame served by Atlassian. Acting as that user, and only within that user's existing Jira permissions, it reads:

  • the issue's description and comments. This content may include names, @mentions and anything users wrote.
  • the user's Jira permissions for that issue, and the issue's edit metadata.
  • the current user's account ID, used to work out which comments the user may edit.

The App converts this content to Markdown and back inside the browser. The content is written back to the same Jira site only when the user presses Save. Everything goes through Atlassian's own APIs, and no copy is sent to us or to anyone else. Atlassian's privacy policy (https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy) and your agreement with Atlassian govern Atlassian's processing.

4. Data we store

None. The App uses no Forge storage, has no backend functions and writes no logs.

5. Sharing, sub-processors and analytics

None. The App has no external network access (no egress). It uses no analytics, tracking, cookies or third-party services of our own.

6. Data location and retention

We hold no App data, so there is nothing to locate, retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes it from your site. Content you saved with it stays in Jira as normal issue content.

7. If you contact us

If you email us, we use what you send (for example your name, email address and message) only to answer you. We keep it no longer than needed to deal with your request.

8. Your rights

The App stores no personal data, so there is no App data to access, correct or erase. For anything about your correspondence with us, contact ozgun@grotesque.ltd. If you are in the UK or EU, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

9. Security

Report security issues to ozgun@grotesque.ltd.

10. Changes

If the App ever starts to store or transmit data, we will update this policy before releasing that version. The effective date above will change whenever we do.
